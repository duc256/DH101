<section class="section">
  <h2>Specific changes I made</h2>

  <div class="grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Clear headings</h3>
      <p>I organized pages with a clear H1, then H2 and H3 sections so the structure is easier to skim and easier for screen readers to follow.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Consistent navigation</h3>
      <p>Each major page links back to the homepage, gallery, reflection, AI-use page, accessibility page, and sustainability page.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Readable design</h3>
      <p>I used larger type, high contrast text, spacious cards, and short sections so the site does not feel crowded or hard to read.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Responsive layout</h3>
      <p>The card grid adjusts on smaller screens, so the site works better on both laptops and phones.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Images and media</h2>
  <p>
    For images and GIFs, I tried to provide surrounding text that explains what the artifact shows and why it matters. For the final version of the site, each image should also include descriptive alt text in Markdown or HTML. Alt text should explain the meaning of the image, not just name the file.
  </p>

  <div class="attribution-box">
    <strong>Example alt text:</strong>
    <p>
      Instead of writing “comic image,” I should write: “Panel from a Tết comic showing a child receiving lucky money during Lunar New Year, used to explore how AI image generation handles cultural storytelling.”
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Known limitations</h2>
  <p>
    Some external tools, such as Google My Maps, Kumu, Google Drive, and Suno, may have accessibility limits I cannot fully control. When possible, I provide a written explanation, screenshot, or direct link so the project is still understandable even if the embed does not work well.
  </p>

    If I had more time, I would run the final website through Lighthouse or axe DevTools and fix any remaining contrast, heading, or alt-text issues.
