<section class="section">
  <h2>What I learned</h2>
  <p>
    My environmental visualization made me think about AI in a more material way. Before this project, I mostly experienced AI as something fast, clean, and convenient. The interface makes it feel weightless. However, the system behind it depends on data centers, electricity, water, hardware, cooling, mining, and global infrastructure.
  </p>

  <p>
    This changed how I think about digital making. A website, an image, a chatbot response, or an AI-generated song can feel small at the user level, but digital systems become environmentally serious at scale. That does not mean I need to stop using technology completely. It means I should stop treating digital work as cost-free.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Concrete steps I took for this site</h2>

  <div class="grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Lightweight pages</h3>
      <p>I kept the site mostly in Markdown, HTML, and CSS instead of using heavy frameworks or unnecessary scripts.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Limited embeds</h3>
      <p>I use direct links and screenshots where possible instead of loading too many external media embeds on one page.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>Image awareness</h3>
      <p>I will compress large images and GIFs when possible so the site loads faster and uses less bandwidth.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3>No trackers</h3>
      <p>I did not add analytics trackers, advertising scripts, or unnecessary third-party tracking code.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Individual and systemic responsibility</h2>
  <p>
    I think individuals have some responsibility to use AI more intentionally, but the largest responsibility belongs to companies, institutions, and policymakers. I can reduce unnecessary prompts or compress images, but I do not control where data centers are built, what energy sources they use, how much water they consume, or how transparent companies are about these systems.
  </p>

  <p>
    This connects to the broader course theme that AI is not only software. It is political, ecological, and economic. A responsible website should therefore not only display my work. It should also ask what hidden systems make that work possible.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Final takeaway</h2>
  <p class="big-quote">
    For me, sustainability means using digital tools with more awareness: not pretending I am outside the system, but refusing to treat convenience as the only value that matters.
  </p>
