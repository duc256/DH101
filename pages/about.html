About me

My name is Duc Tran, and I am a junior at Denison University majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science. I enjoy solving problems and learning how math and technology can work together to explain and improve real-world systems.

Outside of academics, I like playing soccer and spending time with music. I play the guitar and also know a little bit of piano. These hobbies help me stay creative, balanced, and connected with others while managing a busy college schedule.

This portfolio collects my work from DH101: Critical Making in the Age of AI. The site is not only a place to store assignments. It is my final argument about what I learned from making with AI across the semester.

Before this course, I mostly saw AI as a tool for speed: faster images, faster writing, faster ideas, and faster code. I still think AI can be useful, but my view became more complicated. Through the makes, I started to see AI as a system shaped by data, labor, culture, infrastructure, environmental cost, and human judgment.
