Transparency & Ethics

How I Use AI

I used AI in this course, but I do not want to hide that use or pretend the machine did more or less than it actually did.

My basic rule

I used AI as a drafting, generating, brainstorming, and revision tool. I did not treat AI as the final author of my work.

In most projects, AI helped me start faster or test more possibilities. However, I made the final decisions about what counted as successful, what needed to be changed, and how each project connected to the course. This distinction matters because AI can produce polished output without understanding my background, cultural context, ethical concerns, or final purpose.

Tools I used

ChatGPT

I used ChatGPT for brainstorming, organizing reflections, revising sentences for clarity, debugging ideas, and helping me structure pages.

Image generation

I used image generation for visual drafts, including the AI selfie and comic. I selected, edited, and contextualized the final outputs.

Suno

I used Suno to generate music variations from prompt constraints, then curated the outputs based on my own taste and criteria.

Other tools

I also used Canva, Voyant, Kumu, Google My Maps, Twine, Python, Matplotlib, and GitHub Pages.

Concrete examples

AI selfie

AI generated an image based on my prompt, but I did not treat it as an accurate version of myself. I edited the final version to show the gap between my lived identity and the AI-readable version of me.

Tết comic

AI helped generate image drafts, but I changed the story flow, selected emotions, revised dialogue, and fixed the cultural meaning of the sequence.

AI music

Suno generated multiple tracks. I rejected outputs that sounded generic and selected the ones that best matched the intended style.

Writing revision

I used ChatGPT to revise some sentences and organize ideas, but I checked the claims against my actual projects and changed wording that did not sound like me.

Limits, bias, and ethics

The biggest limit of AI in my work was not only obvious mistakes. The more dangerous issue was that AI could produce smooth answers that looked complete while hiding uncertainty, bias, or missing context. In the selfie and comic projects, AI simplified identity and culture into readable patterns. In the labor and ecology projects, I learned that AI also depends on workers, resources, and infrastructures that are often invisible to users.

This is why transparency matters. I want viewers to know where AI helped and where I made decisions. AI can support creative work, but responsible making requires human judgment, citation, correction, and accountability.
