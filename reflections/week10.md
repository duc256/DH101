# Week 10 Reflection

Reflect 10: How does AI change our relationship to play, rules, and narrative?

AI changes our relationship to play, rules, and narrative by making them feel more malleable and interactive, while still being constructed and shaped by systems implicitly. It can adapt to player choices, opening a space for agency and a sense of freedom, but the responses are still based on previous patterns and data, meaning the “rules” are less visible but still influential. It can also conceal how much it shapes answers through social norms; for example, if a profession is prompted for depiction, the AI’s language might perpetuate our societal biases, like assuming it’s male-dominated and using patriarchal language even if the depiction is at our request. This subtly guides the narrative, showing that even in flexible, AI-based play, user choices and outcomes are influenced by underlying societal expectations.
