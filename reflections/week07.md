# Week 7 Reflection

Reflect 7: How does AI reshape global geographies of power, labor, and environment?

AI reshapes global geographies by concentrating power in a few corporate hubs while spreading costs across other places. Decisions about what AI is and how it should work are made in wealthy tech centers, but the compute that runs AI depends on data centers located where land, electricity, and policy make expansion easy, which can increase local energy and water pressures. At the same time, key “invisible” labor like data labeling and content moderation is often outsourced through contractors, making AI look more automated than it is while shifting emotional and social costs onto workers with less power. Overall, AI is not borderless or clean, it reorganizes power, labor, and environmental burdens unevenly across the world.
