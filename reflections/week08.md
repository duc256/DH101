# Week 8 Reflection

Reflect 8: Who is visible/invisible in AI networks? How does visualization reveal or obscure power?

In AI networks, the most visible actors are usually the companies, engineers, and users, while the least visible are the data labelers, affected communities, and workers whose labor makes the system function. This invisibility matters because power often stays with the people who design, own, and control the network, not with those who are most impacted by it. Visualization can reveal power by showing how data, labor, and infrastructure are connected, but it can also hide power when it makes AI seem clean, neutral, or purely technical. A simple diagram may clarify the system, but it can also erase the unequal human relationships behind it.
