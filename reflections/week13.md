# Week 13 Reflection

Reflect 13: Do we imagine AI futures as utopian, dystopian, or something in between? What do those visions reveal about us?

I imagine AI futures as mostly in-between, not fully utopian or dystopian. AI will probably reflect and intensify the systems we already have. If society stays unequal and surveillance-driven, AI may deepen those problems. If we build it with fairness and accountability, it could also improve daily life. These visions reveal a lot about us today. Utopian futures show what we hope for, while dystopian futures show what we fear, like loss of privacy, control, and human connection. So when we imagine AI futures, we are really revealing our current values, anxieties, and social problems.
