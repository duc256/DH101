# Week 12 Reflection

Reflect 12: Is AI sustainable? What ecological trade-offs are we willing to accept?

I do not think AI is fully sustainable in the way it is currently growing. It depends on a lot of electricity, water, hardware, and extraction, so even though it feels invisible and clean to users, it has real environmental costs. I think AI can become more sustainable than it is now, but only if companies are more transparent, use cleaner energy, design more efficient systems, and stop treating constant growth as automatically good.

As for trade-offs, I think some are easier to justify than others. I am more willing to accept environmental costs when AI is used for things that have clear social value, like education, accessibility, medical research, or solving important problems. I am less willing to accept those costs when AI is used mainly for convenience, profit, or wasteful overuse. So for me, the real question is not just whether AI is sustainable, but whether the benefits are important enough to justify the environmental burden and whether that burden is being shared fairly.
