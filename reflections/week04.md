# Week 4 Reflection

Reflect 4: Is AI a collaborator, tool, or plagiarist in storytelling?

AI is mostly a tool, sometimes a collaborator, and it becomes “plagiarism-like” only when someone uses AI output as the final work and claims it as fully their own without credit. For my Tết comic, AI helped generate images and draft dialogue fast, but it did not understand the culture or the lesson. I had to make the real storytelling decisions: fixing repeated faces, keeping outfits consistent, making the boy’s emotion clear, and making sure the speech bubbles came from the right people. That is why I see AI as support, not authorship. AI feels like a collaborator when it gives ideas I can build on, but the human still decides what is accurate, respectful, and meaningful.
