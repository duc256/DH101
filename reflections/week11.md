# Week 11 Reflection

Reflect 11: Who does the invisible work of AI, and who profits from it?

The invisible work of AI is mostly done by people like data labelers, content moderators, and other contract workers who prepare data and keep systems running. They often work for low pay and receive little recognition, while large tech companies and investors make most of the profit. So even though AI looks automatic, it still depends on human labor that is kept out of sight.
