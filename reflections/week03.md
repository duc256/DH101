# Week 3 Reflection

Reflect 3: What does “authenticity” mean when identity is co-constructed with AI?

Authenticity, when identity is co-constructed with AI, means the identity is not coming from one "true" source anymore. It is shaped by both the person and the system. AI does not just reflect who someone is, it interprets them through patterns, categories, and assumptions built into its training data and design. So authenticity become less about perfect accuracy and more about transparency and agency: being clear that the result is a collaboration, understanding what the AI is adding or changing, and deciding whther the final representation still matches how the person wants to be seen. In this context, something can feel authentic if it communicates a real part of someone's identity, even if it is not a literal copy, as long as the person has control over the choices and the limits are acknowledged.
