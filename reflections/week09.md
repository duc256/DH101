# Week 9 Reflection

Reflect 9: What is creativity when AI can generate endlessly? Where is the human in generative work?

When AI can endlessly generate content, "creativity" basically turns into being a good editor. The human isn't physically making the art anymore but we are just the filter deciding what's actually good. My experience building this felt more like giving directions than actually driving. I wasn't in full control of the output—I just set the general vibe and parameters, and the AI handled all the actual notes and vocals. Generating the tracks was easy, but the real work was dealing with the "exhaustion of novelty." Clicking through dozens of boring, generic tracks just to find one that felt authentic was surprisingly tiring. Ultimately, the algorithm does the heavy lifting, but the human provides the taste.
