# Week 6 Reflection

Reflect 6: What do we gain/lose when machines “read” literature for us?

We gain speed, scale, and pattern recognition when machines “read” literature for us. Tools like Voyant can quickly show repetition, word frequency, and distribution that might take much longer to notice by hand, while GPT can generate interpretations about theme, tone, and structure in seconds. This can help us start analysis faster and ask better questions.

What we lose is the slower, more personal process of reading and judging meaning ourselves. Machines can flatten ambiguity: Voyant reduces a text to patterns, and GPT can produce confident interpretations without always showing evidence. If we rely on them too much, we may stop noticing how interpretation is made. So the best use of machine reading is not to replace human reading, but to support it.
