# Week 5 Reflection

Reflect 5: How does AI alter authorship and remix culture? Who owns AI-made art?

AI changes authorship because “making” becomes prompting, selecting, editing, not one person creating from scratch. It also supercharges remix culture by generating endless variations from existing styles and images. Who owns AI-made art is complicated: legally, fully AI-generated work may not get full copyright protection because copyright usually expects human authorship, and platform terms also matter. Ethically, the strongest claim goes to whoever made the key creative choices and shaped the final result, but AI makes authorship feel shared and blurry.
