# Week 2 Reflection

Reflect 2: Can AI help us define what it means to be human, or does it reduce us to patterns?

I think that AI can help us define what it means to be human. When AI are capable of writing, creating, and solving problems, it shows that some intelligence is pattern-based and repeatable. That forces us to define humanity in a deeper way than just skills or productivity. What stands out is that human have lived experience, emotions, relationships, and moral responsibility. AI can imitate empathy or meaning but it does not actually feel pain, guilt, or love and it does not live with consequences. Eventhough AI can imitate parts of human behavior, it can not fully capture what it is like to be a person. In that way, AI does not erase humanity, it pushes us to define it more clearly as something deeper than patterns.
